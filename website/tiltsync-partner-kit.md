# TiltSync Partner Kit

## About TiltSync

TiltSync is a mobile app that helps installers and DIYers align solar panels in the field. Using your phone's sensors, it provides real-time tilt and compass readings with haptic feedback to dial in the perfect angle — no experience required.

## Key Features

- **Real-time tilt measurement** — Use your phone's sensors to measure panel tilt angle with precision.
- **Compass alignment** — Verify azimuth orientation to maximize solar exposure.
- **Multiple tilt formulas** — Choose from industry-standard calculations including latitude-based rules and seasonal adjustments.
- **NREL PVWatts integration** — Optional live data from the National Renewable Energy Laboratory for location-specific optimal angles.
- **Haptic feedback** — Feel when you've hit the target angle, so you can keep your eyes on the panel.

## Target Audience

Solar installers, DIY homeowners, off-grid enthusiasts, and anyone setting up solar panels who wants to get the angle right the first time.

## How We Complement SunTrace3D

SunTrace3D helps users understand the roof — visualizing shadows, placing virtual panels, and estimating yield before installation. TiltSync picks up where that planning ends: on the roof, rail in hand, dialing in the precise tilt and azimuth. Together, we cover the full workflow from analysis to alignment.
